Native inc 14+15: onboarding UI + NVR multi-channel (P2-01/02/05/13)

The operator-facing device-management surface (owner item #3, sequenced
after ONVIF and device health), plus the recorder onboarding workflow that
completes P2-01. Landed as one commit because both are the same cohesive
feature — the Devices tab — and were built back-to-back without an
intervening commit.

inc 14 — Onboarding UI:
  - DeviceController (src/workspace/, Qt) bridges the two pure cores to QML,
    mirroring PlaybackController: DeviceRepo (atomic onboard / idempotent
    channel sync / remove over the P0-04 store + DPAPI SecretStore under the
    broker's credential_ref scheme; new listDevices() accessor) and
    HealthMonitor (honest per-device reach/stream/storage -> derived state,
    exception raise -> acknowledge -> auto-clear, maintenance windows).
  - A Devices tab in vms_workspace (DevicesView.qml): device list with honest
    health badges (green Online / amber Degraded / red Offline / slate
    Unsupported / grey Unknown-until-observed), an onboard form, and
    per-device acknowledge / maintenance / remove. The credential secret is
    only ever passed into onboard() — never read back or shown.
  - Health is Unknown-until-observed; real observations from broker connect
    outcomes + ONVIF polling are the live-blocked remainder (the
    reportReach/Stream/Storage methods are the wiring points).

inc 15 — NVR/hybrid multi-channel onboarding:
  - Schema v5 adds devices.kind (camera | nvr | dvr | hybrid), a forward-only
    additive migration. DeviceOnboard/DeviceSummary carry kind.
  - DeviceController::onboardRecorder expands a channel count + per-channel
    RTSP URL templates ({ch} -> the 1-based channel number) into N channels
    with stable ids (<id>-ch<N>), onboarded atomically with the device, its
    default group, and its credential.
  - The form gains a device-kind selector (recorder kinds show channel-count
    + template fields); cards show kind + channel count.
